About PaydaySuperGuard

PaydaySuperGuard is a compliance and reconciliation review tool built for accountants, BAS agents, payroll professionals, and small-business advisers. Payday Super applies from 1 July 2026, and super contributions generally need to be received by the employee's fund within 7 business days of payday (longer in some cases, such as new employees or new funds). Upload a payroll export and it reviews every payment, sorts on-time from problem records, and estimates potential Super Guarantee Charge (SGC) exposure — so you can review before it becomes a compliance problem, not after.

Every check runs as plain date-and-arithmetic logic in your browser — no artificial intelligence is involved in producing the flags or figures.

Built by DashCraftCo, an independent maker of practical, privacy-first tools.
